Hanayome Noren, Nanao, Ishikawa (石川県七尾　花嫁のれん)    29 October, 2022

"'Hanayome Noren', or bridal curtain, is a custom started in the 1800s that still goes on in Noto, Kaga, and Ecchu regions of the former Kaga Domain. On the day of the wedding, the curtain is hung at the entrance of the Buddhist alter room of the groom’s house for the bride to pass through. Mostly made using a traditional Kaga-yuzen technique, these expensive and gorgeous curtains are an exclusive luxury used only once and are usually put in a chest of drawers after the wedding" (Hanayome Noren Museum by ANA).  See also 花嫁のれんとは .
